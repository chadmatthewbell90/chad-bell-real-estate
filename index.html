Seller questions

Thinking of selling?

Straight answers to what sellers ask me most. Anything else, just WhatsApp me.

What is my home worth?

That depends on your home, your street and what's sold nearby recently. The best way to know is a free valuation: I come to you, look at the property properly and show you the recent sales the price is based on.

Does a valuation commit me to selling?

No. A valuation is free and there's no obligation. Plenty of homeowners get one to plan ahead. If you decide to sell, we'll talk about the mandate then.

How is your marketing different?

Most listings rely on portal photos. Your home also gets video and reels built for social media, shared with an audience that already follows property on The Bluff. Buyers see it on their phones, share it with their partner, and book a viewing.

How long will it take to sell?

It depends mostly on price, condition and the market at the time. A home priced right from the start sells faster than one that has to be reduced later, which is why I'm honest about price at the valuation.

Which certificates do I need to sell?

In KwaZulu-Natal you'll need an electrical certificate of compliance, plus a gas certificate and an electric fence certificate if your home has those installed. Buyers and banks on the coast usually also ask for a beetle (entomologist's) certificate.

I'll tell you exactly what your property needs and put you in touch with reliable local inspectors.

What does it cost to sell?

My commission is agreed upfront and in writing before anything goes live, so there are no surprises. Other costs to plan for are the compliance certificates and, if you have a home loan, the bond cancellation costs. I'll walk you through all of it at the valuation.

Sole mandate or open mandate?

With a sole mandate, one agent is fully responsible for selling your home for an agreed period, which means a full marketing campaign and one consistent asking price. With an open mandate, several agents can sell it. I'll explain the trade-offs for your situation honestly.
