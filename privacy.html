Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how Chad Bell Real Estate ("I", "me") handles personal information shared through this website, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What I collect

This website does not store anything you type into it. When you use the valuation form, your browser opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp yourself. The details are:

  • your name and phone number
  • the street or suburb of your property, its type and when you'd like to sell
  • anything you add in the notes field

If you contact me by phone, WhatsApp or email instead, I receive whatever you choose to share.

How I use it

Only to reply to you, arrange a valuation and, if you choose to sell with me, market and sell your property. I don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Who else sees it

  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries messages you send through WhatsApp, under its own privacy policy.
  • Acutts Bluff, the agency I work through, may hold your details in its client records if you go ahead with a valuation or a mandate.
  • If you sell, the conveyancing attorney and others needed to complete the transfer receive what the sale requires.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tracking. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them. If tracking is added in future, this policy will be updated first.

Your rights

You can ask what personal information I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to me using it. Contact me on chadbell@acutts.co.za or 084 520 6603. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).
